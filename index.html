About Bishar Graphics

Dedicated to Precision Garment Branding

BISHAR GRAPHICS MANUFACTURER specializes in custom heat-transfer stickers, screen printing, and complete garment labeling solutions. We cater directly to clothing manufacturers, private apparel brands, and custom uniform providers.

Our transfers are crafted to combine exact aesthetic replication with enduring wash-fastness, utilizing specialized inks formulated for long-term textile adhesion without cracking or fading.
